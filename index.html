    Featured projects

    Project 01

    Multi-channel AI agent inside GoHighLevel

    Every DM, SMS and WhatsApp message gets an answer in seconds, a booking when the person is ready and a human when it matters.

    Problem
    Messages arrived after hours across five channels, and replies came hours later.
    Trigger
    Any inbound message fires the GHL "Receive and Process DMs" workflow, which batches rapid messages into one payload for n8n.
    AI decision
    A router picks the Website, Engagement or Follow-up agent. Each has its own knowledge and memory, and decides to reply, book or escalate.
    Result
    8.76 second measured reply, bookings written to the calendar, pipeline stages updated and clean hand-offs to staff.

    GoHighLevel workflows, n8n, LeadConnector API, OpenRouter, Supabase pgvector, Postgres memory
